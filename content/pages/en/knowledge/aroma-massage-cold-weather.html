Aroma Massage in Cold Weather: Why Therapists Recommend It

7 November 2026 | Sukhoayothaya Massage & Spa Khon Kaen

We recommend a relaxing aromatic oil massage during the cool weather for ultimate relaxation ..

Why do therapists so often recommend aroma massage in the cool season or at the end of the year when the weather turns cold? Let's look at the reasons..

1. Relaxes muscles tensed up by the cold
Eases aches and stiffness: When it's cold, the body tends to tense up without us noticing, making aches and stiffness in the neck, shoulders or back more likely.
Boosts blood circulation: Warm oil combined with massage helps warm the body, dilating blood vessels so blood circulates better.

2. Nourishes dry, rough skin
Restores moisture: Cool weather often comes with dryness, which makes skin lose moisture and become dry and rough easily. The essential oils or nourishing balms used in the massage help coat and replenish moisture, leaving skin smooth and soft again.

3. Relieves stress and adds emotional warmth
Aromatherapy: The scent of essential oils stimulates the senses, balances mood, and helps reduce the stress or anxiety that often rises in cool weather.
Helps you sleep well: Warmth and pleasant scents ease fatigue, helping the body feel relaxed so you sleep more soundly.

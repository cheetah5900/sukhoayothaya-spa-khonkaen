Herbal Steam for 10-15 Minutes Before a Massage: The Benefits

4 November 2026 | Sukhoayothaya Massage & Spa Khon Kaen

A 10-15 minute herbal steam before your massage prepares your body and makes the massage more effective

• Muscles relax Moist heat makes muscles and tendons more flexible, so the massage reaches tight spots more deeply with less discomfort
• Better circulation Heat dilates blood vessels, helping reduce fatigue, aches and stiffness
• Opens pores and promotes sweating Helps the body feel light and lets the skin absorb massage oil better
• Herbal scents help you relax Such as lemongrass, plai, turmeric, camphor and tamarind leaves — they calm the mind, clear the airways and relieve stress
• Properties of the herbs For example, plai helps reduce inflammation and aches, and camphor helps clear the nose
• Relieves aches and pains Ideal for people who sit for long hours, have office syndrome, or play sports

*Not a medical treatment. If symptoms are severe, please see a doctor. Results vary from person to person.*
